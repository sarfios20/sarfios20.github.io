A kinematic bicycle model seen from above. You push the car with the mouse and it only moves along the arc its wheels allow, so the rear wheels track inside the front ones exactly the way they do in real life. It traces both axle paths, the body swept path and the instantaneous turning center, across scenarios like turning past a parked car or reverse parallel parking. Pure JavaScript on canvas, no dependencies and no server.
